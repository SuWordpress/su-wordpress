Ask Me on WhatsApp
01  What services do you offer?+
I offer WordPress website design (from scratch or templates), custom feature development, WooCommerce store setup, and fixing any WordPress errors or issues your website is facing.
02  How long does it take to build a website?+
A standard WordPress website typically takes 3–7 days depending on complexity. A simple landing page can be done in 1–2 days. I'll give you a clear timeline before we start.
03  Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?+
Yes! I work with clients worldwide through Fiverr and direct contact. I communicate clearly and am flexible with time zones to ensure smooth project delivery.
04  Can you fix my existing WordPress website?+
Absolutely. I handle all kinds of WordPress errors — white screen of death, plugin conflicts, slow loading, security issues, broken layouts, and more. Just describe the problem and I'll fix it.
05  What information do you need to start?+
I'll need your business name, what you do, your preferred design style (or reference sites), any content/images you have, and your goals for the website. We'll discuss everything before I begin.
06  How do I pay for your services?+
You can hire me directly through Fiverr (safest option with buyer protection) or we can arrange payment through other methods. I'll always discuss pricing transparently before any work begins.
